🧠 Human Brain

Advantages

Limitations

  • Emotional bias

💻 Artificial Intelligence

Advantages

  • Extremely fast
  • Accurate calculations
  • Processes massive data
  • Available 24/7
  • Automates repetitive tasks

Limitations

  • No emotions
  • No consciousness

  • Depends on data quality
  • Can inherit bias
